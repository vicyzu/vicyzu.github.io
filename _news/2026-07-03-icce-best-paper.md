<h2>Best Paper Award</h2>

<p>
VIC Lab received the <strong>Best Paper Award</strong> for the paper entitled:
</p>

<div class="news-paper-title">
<strong>
“Adaptive Transfer Learning Strategies for a Vision Transformer-Based Seismic Foundation Model.”
</strong>
</div>

<p>
The research proposes adaptive transfer learning strategies for improving Vision Transformer-based foundation models in seismic interpretation. The award recognizes the novelty, technical contribution, and potential impact of the work in intelligent geoscience.
</p>

<h2>International Collaboration</h2>

<p>

Our laboratory presented a total of <strong>three research papers</strong> at ICCE-TW 2026. These studies were conducted through collaborations between <strong>Yuan Ze University (Taiwan)</strong>, <strong>Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (Malaysia)</strong>, <strong>Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (Malaysia)</strong>, and <strong>Kasetsart University (Thailand)</strong>.

</p>

<p>

These collaborations demonstrate VIC Lab's commitment to conducting internationally collaborative research and developing AI technologies for real-world scientific and engineering applications.

</p>
