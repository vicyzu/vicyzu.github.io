VIC Lab participated in the **COSITE Conference 2025**, held in **Indonesia**, where our members presented **two research papers** in the fields of computer vision and artificial intelligence. One of the presented papers, *"Zero-Shot Representation Learning for Alzheimer's Disease MRI via Frozen CLIP,"* received the **Best Paper Award**. The award was presented to **Chun-Yi Lin** in recognition of the paper's technical contribution and research quality.
