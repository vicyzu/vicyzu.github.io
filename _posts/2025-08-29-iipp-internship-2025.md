VIC Lab hosted international students through the **International Industrial Partnership Program (IIPP)** and the **Taiwan Experience Education Program (TEEP)**, providing research training in computer vision, artificial intelligence, and intelligent computing.

Throughout their internships, participants worked closely with VIC Lab members on research projects, attended laboratory meetings, and gained hands-on experience in developing AI-based solutions. These programs also promoted cultural exchange and strengthened international academic collaboration.

We sincerely thank all internship participants for their enthusiasm, dedication, and valuable contributions.
