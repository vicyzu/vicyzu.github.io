VIC Lab participated in the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics–Taiwan (ICCE-TW 2026), held in Taoyuan, Taiwan. During the conference, our laboratory presented three collaborative research papers with international partners from Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (Malaysia)** and **Kasetsart University (Thailand).

One of our papers, entitled "Adaptive Transfer Learning Strategies for a Vision Transformer-Based Seismic Foundation Model," received the **Best Paper Award**, recognizing the quality and impact of the research.

The conference also provided valuable opportunities to strengthen existing collaborations, establish new academic connections, and exchange research ideas with colleagues from universities and research institutions around the world.
