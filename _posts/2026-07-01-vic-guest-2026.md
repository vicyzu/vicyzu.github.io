VIC Lab welcomed several visiting professors from partner universities to our laboratory. These visits provided valuable opportunities for academic exchange, research discussions, and the exploration of future collaborative projects.

During their visits, the professors met with VIC Lab members to discuss shared research interests, exchange ideas on future joint research, and explore opportunities for student exchange and international academic collaboration. These interactions further strengthened VIC Lab's global research network and laid the foundation for future collaborative activities.
